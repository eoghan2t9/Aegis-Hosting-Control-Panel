Aegis is licensed under the [PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](LICENSE).
That licence is **free for noncommercial use** and **does not allow commercial
use**. If you want to use Aegis commercially, you need a separate paid licence.

This page is a plain-language guide. It is not the licence — if it ever
disagrees with [`LICENSE`](LICENSE), the `LICENSE` file wins.

## Free (noncommercial)

You do not need to ask or pay for uses such as:

- running Aegis on your own server for personal projects, a homelab or a hobby site;
- learning from it, research, experiments, testing and evaluation;
- use by charities, schools, public research and safety/health organisations,
  and government institutions — the licence's "noncommercial organization" terms
  apply to them.

You may change the code for these purposes. See [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md)
— we ask that you contribute your changes back.

## Needs a commercial licence

Anything aimed at commercial advantage or monetary compensation, for example:

- hosting **paying customers** with Aegis (a hosting company, agency or reseller);
- running it as part of a business's infrastructure or product;
- bundling, reselling or offering it as a paid or managed service.

If you are unsure which side you are on, ask before you deploy.

## How to get a commercial licence

Open a **GitHub issue or discussion** on this repository titled
**"Commercial licence"** and describe how you plan to use Aegis (roughly how many
servers and customer accounts). Price and terms are agreed per licence, in
writing. Commercial licences are granted on terms that may include contributing
your changes back to this repository.

Please do not post private business details in a public issue; say you would
like to discuss them and a private channel will be arranged.

## Versions released under MIT

Copies of Aegis obtained **before** the licence change remain available to you
under the MIT licence that applied to them. The change applies to the code as
published from that point on.
